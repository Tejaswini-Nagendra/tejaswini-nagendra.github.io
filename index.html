About Dasara

Mysore Dasara, known as Nada Habba, is Karnataka's state festival celebrated for 10 days. The nine days are observed as Navaratri and the tenth day as Vijayadashami, making the victory of good over evil.

Events

Palace Events

Live music, light & sound show, classical dance, and royal ceremonies held at the Mysore Palace courtyard.

Other Events

Food fair, folk performances, handicrafts markets, and art exhibitions across Doddakere Maidan. Explore local flavors and crafts.
